FIVE TEACHER ACCOUNT SETUP TEMPLATE
===================================
Firebase Authentication internal email pattern:
91 + 10-digit mobile + @login.ezeevisionchampua.com

Create 5 accounts manually in Firebase Authentication. For each Authentication user,
copy its UID and create users/{UID} in Firestore with:

name       (string)     Teacher full name
mobile     (string)     10-digit mobile only
role       (string)     teacher
status     (string)     approved
createdAt  (timestamp)  current date/time
updatedAt  (timestamp)  current date/time

Do not store teacher passwords in this file, Firestore, GitHub, WhatsApp, or spreadsheets.
All 5 trusted teachers have identical permissions.
